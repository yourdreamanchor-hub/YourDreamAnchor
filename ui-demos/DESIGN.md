# UI proposals for comparison

Scope: the two alternative HTML layouts in this directory. Both were requested for comparison. The user chose to retain the original dark-and-gold site, so these layouts remain archived proposals. The other preview pages display the accepted refinements to the original website.

## The Wedding Edit

Warm editorial direction for couples and families. Ivory `#f6f1e8`, deeper paper `#ebe3d6`, wine `#6b3049`, forest green `#263d34`, ink `#2c342d`, secondary text `#606257`. Fraunces display text at 350–400 weight, Manrope for reading and controls. Three-pixel corners on controls and photography; the main photo has a tall rounded frame echoing the mandap. One restrained opening photo reveal, no repeated scroll animations.

The first viewport uses a personal photograph composition, a legible offer, a date enquiry action, and a playable celebration. Reels come before the longer biography. Ceremonies read as a list, followed by family comments and a simple inquiry form. Phone layouts have a persistent booking action. Signature interaction: ceremony selection carries into the enquiry form.

## After Hours

A cinematic direction carrying the energy of live hosting. Warm charcoal `#141410`, panels `#20201a`, champagne `#dbbb77`, ivory `#f5efe1`, secondary text `#b6b4a5`. Fraunces at low softness and large optical size, Manrope for controls. Square photography and two-pixel control corners. A film stage is separated from the headline so moving images do not compete with it.

Reels arrive before the biography; a featured reel gives the section an asymmetrical rhythm. Two large event photos accompany an open ceremony list. A large couple quote leads into a champagne enquiry section. Signature interaction: the opening date and city controls carry into the inquiry form. Background video respects reduced motion and pauses when offscreen.

## Shared mechanics

Local media and fonts; no remote layout dependencies. Visible keyboard focus and native form controls. Native dialog with video controls and Escape dismissal. Filter buttons expose pressed states and update a live result count. The comparison page offers both 1440px desktop and 390px phone frames. Form submission produces a truthful local preview; it sends no data and does not claim to check availability.
