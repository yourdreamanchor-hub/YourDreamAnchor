# Your Dream Anchor — comparison demos

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose and scope

The user requested an assessment of the existing UI and runnable HTML demos of improvements. They selected both a warm editorial direction and a refined cinematic direction for comparison, then chose to retain the original dark-and-gold site. These two alternatives are archived local review artifacts. The live-site preview pages show the accepted refinements to the original design.

## Product and evidence

The existing README, homepage, and seed content identify Your Dream Anchor as the website for Akshay R Takalkar, a wedding and event anchor. Existing services include haldi and mehendi, sangeet, weddings and varmala, games, receptions, and live music. The current site provides real event photos, playable reels, couple comments, and a date enquiry workflow with a WhatsApp alternative.

The demos reuse existing photos and reels from ../media and copy from ../src/seed.ts. They must preserve the existing name and public contact information, avoid invented statistics, ratings, testimonials, or booking availability, and identify the enquiry form as a local demonstration. No enquiry may be transmitted from the demo form.

## Audience and task

Inferred from the existing homepage: couples and families considering an anchor for their celebration want to see hosting work, understand the ceremonies covered, and ask about their date.

## Constraints

Plain HTML with inline styles and scripts, local bundled fonts and media, responsive desktop and phone layouts, keyboard accessible controls, reduced motion support. The two alternative layouts are archived proposals. Separate Hero, Games and scroll preview pages embed the existing Next.js/Payload application on port 3000.
